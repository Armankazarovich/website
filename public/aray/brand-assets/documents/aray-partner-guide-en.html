ARAY Partner Guide

A practical guide for partners who sell and support ARAY / Yuva marketing services in their region.

Version
2026.06.03
Language
English
Status
Draft Standard
Owner
ARAY / Yuva

Partner Role

The partner finds clients, explains the ARAY / Yuva offer, collects the business brief and keeps the relationship with the client.

Production Role

ARAY / Yuva performs the approved marketing work: website, PWA app, SEO, advertising, PR, branding, automation and AI implementation.

Workflow

1

Lead

The partner finds a business owner and explains the offer.

2

Brief

The client provides business information, goals, assets and access details.

3

Invoice

The partner issues an invoice to the client according to their legal status.

4

Production

After payment is confirmed, the ARAY / Yuva team starts the production order.

Partner Share

The pilot model is 50/50: the client pays 150 000 RUB per month, and the partner pays 75 000 RUB to ARAY / Yuva for production.

Quality Rule

The partner must use only approved scripts, tariffs, brand materials and advertising wording from the ARAY brand kit.
