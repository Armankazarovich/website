ARAY Payments And Requisites

This document defines the safe payment flow, partner data checklist and the rule for storing real requisites.

Version
2026.06.03
Language
English
Status

Model
invoices and contracts

Base Flow

The partner issues an invoice to the client from their own company or sole proprietor status. After receiving payment, the partner pays ARAY / Yuva for production.

Pilot Economy

The client pays 150 000 RUB per month. The partner pays 75 000 RUB to ARAY / Yuva for production and support under the approved order.

Recipient Profile

Received

IE Vardanyan Araik Yurievich

A private requisites profile has been prepared for accountant review and future invoice template integration.

Not Public

Bank Details Are Hidden

Settlement account, tax ID, bank code and other requisites are not published in open documents or brand materials.

Data Checklist

Partner

Partner Data

Legal name, legal status, tax ID, address, bank details, accountant contact, country and working city.

ARAY

Recipient Data

Company or sole proprietor, tax ID, bank account, bank name, bank code, payment purpose, contract and responsible contact.

Client

Client Data

Legal name, tax ID, requisites, contact, service, billing period, amount and invoice basis.

Security

Where To Store

Real requisites must be stored only in a private admin panel, database or protected organization profile.

Do Not Publish

Real bank details, contracts, personal IDs, access credentials and tax documents must not be published in public brand materials.

Next Step

After requisites are approved, a private organization profile and invoice template are created. The public brandbook keeps only the instruction without sensitive data.
