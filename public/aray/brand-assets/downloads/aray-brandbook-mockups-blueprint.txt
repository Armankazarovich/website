ARAY Brandbook + Mockup Library Blueprint
Updated: 2026-06-03

Goal:
Create one approved brand system for ARAY / Yuva partners, managers and bloggers.

Logo system:
- Official ARAY A Mark: original Corel colors for documents, print and partner materials.
- Live ARAY A Mark: adaptive palette version for ARAY admin, apps and digital themes.
- Mono Light: white/silver version for dark surfaces.
- Mono Dark: black/graphite version for light surfaces and simple print.

Brandbook sections:
- Logo usage and clear space
- Official colors and live palette logic
- Typography rules
- Background rules
- Do / do not examples
- Partner wording
- Advertising rules
- Document style
- Print style
- Social media style

Required mockups:
- Client presentation
- Commercial proposal
- A4 / A5 flyer
- Business card
- Partner badge
- Website banner desktop
- Website banner mobile
- Social post 1080x1080
- Story 1080x1920
- Telegram / VK post image
- QR lead card
- Invoice / act / contract visual cover

Production rule:
Only approved files from the ARAY brand kit can be used by partners.
Draft layouts must be reviewed before printing, publishing or sending to clients.
