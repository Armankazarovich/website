ARAY Partner Advertising Rules
Updated: 2026-06-03

Positioning:
ARAY / Yuva gives the client a marketing department under key for the monthly budget of one marketer.

Allowed wording:
- Marketing under key: website, PWA app, SEO, advertising, PR, branding, automation and AI implementation.
- The partner represents ARAY / Yuva services in their region and keeps client communication.
- The ARAY / Yuva production team performs the marketing work according to the approved tariff, brief and contract.
- The base pilot tariff is 150 000 RUB per month.

Not allowed:
- Do not promise guaranteed profit, guaranteed sales or guaranteed advertising result.
- Do not present ARAY as an investment, coin, bank, wallet, MLM or financial product.
- Do not promise ARC withdrawal or ARC buyback.
- Do not change the price, scope or terms without approval.
- Do not distort the logo, recolor the official version manually, stretch it or place it on a noisy background.

Partner rule:
The partner uses only approved brand materials from the ARAY brand kit.
All client promises must match the tariff, invoice, contract and current production capacity.
